Temporal tri-planes

what the deformation field stores, played over continuous time

The field encodes space as three 128×128 planes of 32 channels each, and time as a table of 6 weights at 75 knots. A plane at time t is base + Σr wr(t)·resr — affine in those weights, so the whole timeline reconstructs in the browser from 7 small images per plane. It is exact at every knot; between them the weights are blended straight, which is the grid's own continuous-time path. (The trained renderer takes a different route between knots — it reads the field at both and cubic-Hermite interpolates the predicted offsets, which is what the velocity heads are for.) Everything is masked to the cells the canonical cloud actually reaches: the rest of the plane is never supervised, so its values are arbitrary — and large enough to dominate both the projection and the display range if you let them.
