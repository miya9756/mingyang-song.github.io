Knot count as a regularizer

A casual hand-held capture (HyperNeRF americano) — the unreliable case, where the input barely constrains the motion. Cutting the number of spline knots removes degrees of freedom from the trajectory, which stabilizes the reconstruction much as the loss terms do, at the cost of some high-frequency detail. The third panel shows what the full knot count does with no regularizers at all.

These three are rendered square and wider than the capture camera, which was a 536×960 portrait frame. You are seeing well outside the filmed frustum, floaters included — that is the point: it shows what the reconstruction actually is in 3D, rather than replaying the one view that was supervised.
