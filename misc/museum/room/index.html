The Painted Room

Gilded things, borrowed light, and water over a sunken floor.

Building the room…
drag to look around scroll or pinch to step closer click anywhere to aim the lamp and the water answers

The five paintings are my own photographs, repainted by Oil Paint Tuner. The room takes its atmosphere from my illustration, 2023. The ruin they are lost in is drawn with three.js on WebGPU, falling back to WebGL2 where that is not available.

AI contributor: GPT-6 Astra (via Codex) — scene development, geometry, and code refinement under my artistic direction.
