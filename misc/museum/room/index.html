Artist’s note

The five paintings are my own photographs, repainted by Oil Paint Tuner. The room takes its atmosphere from my illustration, 2023.

The ruin is drawn with three.js on WebGPU, with a WebGL2 fallback. AI contributor GPT-6 Astra (via Codex) worked on scene development, geometry, and code refinement under my artistic direction.
